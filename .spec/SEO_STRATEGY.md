# SEO_STRATEGY.md: Nureaknite

## 1. SEO Goals

The primary SEO goals for Nureaknite are to:
*   Increase organic search visibility for relevant knitting, crochet, and craft-related keywords.
*   Drive qualified organic traffic to the website, leading to increased engagement with content (blog, tutorials, patterns).
*   Generate leads for coaching services.
*   Establish Nureaknite as an authoritative and trusted personal brand within the knitting and crochet community.

## 2. Keyword Strategy

### 2.1. Core Keyword Research
Identify keywords across different user intents:
*   **Informational:** "how to knit a scarf," "crochet stitches for beginners," "best yarn for blankets," "knitting vs crochet."
*   **Navigational:** "Nureaknite patterns," "Nureaknite blog."
*   **Commercial Investigation:** "easy knitting patterns free," "crochet kit for beginners."
*   **Local:** "private knitting coach [location]."

### 2.2. Keyword Categories
*   **Brand Keywords:** "Nureaknite," "Nureaknite patterns," "Nureaknite crochet."
*   **Craft-Specific Keywords:** "knitting patterns," "crochet tutorials," "yarn types," "stitch guides."
*   **Product/Service Keywords:** "crochet patterns free," "knitting kits," "handmade knitwear," "offline knitting coaching [city/region]."
*   **Long-Tail Keywords:** Focus on specific problems or niche interests, e.g., "how to fix dropped stitch knitting," "beginner friendly amigurumi pattern," "sustainable yarn brands Indonesia."

### 2.3. Tools & Methodology
*   Utilize tools like Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, SEMrush, or Ubersuggest for keyword discovery, volume, and difficulty analysis.
*   Analyze competitor keywords and content.
*   Monitor search trends related to knitting and crochet.

## 3. On-Page SEO

### 3.1. Content Optimization
*   **Blog & Tutorials:** Each article will target specific keywords, providing comprehensive answers to user queries. Content will be structured with clear headings (H1, H2, H3), bullet points, and internal links.
*   **Pattern Library:** Each pattern page will have a unique, descriptive title, meta description, and rich content including difficulty level, craft type, materials, and clear instructions.
*   **Product Showcase:** Product descriptions for kits, tools, and finished works will be detailed, using relevant keywords for materials, style, and use cases.
*   **Portfolio Showcase:** Descriptions for each project will include keywords related to the technique, item type, and design inspiration.
*   **About Page:** Optimized to convey expertise and brand story, using keywords related to "knitting designer," "crochet educator," "craft content creator."

### 3.2. Technical On-Page Elements
*   **Title Tags:** Unique, descriptive, and keyword-rich for every page (max 60 characters).
*   **Meta Descriptions:** Compelling summaries that encourage clicks, including target keywords (max 160 characters).
*   **Header Tags (H1, H2, H3):** Logical content hierarchy, with H1 for the main topic and H2/H3 for sub-topics, incorporating keywords.
*   **Image Alt Text:** Descriptive alt text for all images, including relevant keywords for accessibility and image search.
*   **URL Structure:** Clean, human-readable, and keyword-friendly URLs (e.g., `/patterns/easy-crochet-scarf`, `/blog/how-to-knit-socks`).
*   **Internal Linking:** Strategically link related blog posts, patterns, and products to improve site navigation, distribute link equity, and keep users engaged.
*   **Schema Markup (Structured Data):** Implement schema.org markup for:
    *   `Product` for patterns and shop items.
    *   `BlogPosting` for blog articles.
    *   `Organization` for the Nureaknite brand.
    *   `LocalBusiness` for offline coaching (if applicable).

## 4. Technical SEO

### 4.1. Site Speed & Performance
*   Leverage Next.js's capabilities for Server-Side Rendering (SSR) and Static Site Generation (SSG) to ensure fast page load times.
*   Optimize images (compression, lazy loading, appropriate formats like WebP).
*   Minimize JavaScript and CSS.
*   Utilize Vercel's CDN for fast content delivery.
*   Monitor Core Web Vitals (LCP, FID, CLS) using Google Search Console.

### 4.2. Mobile-Friendliness
*   Ensure the website is fully responsive and provides an optimal user experience across all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile).

### 4.3. Crawlability & Indexability
*   **XML Sitemaps:** Generate and submit an XML sitemap to Google Search Console to help search engines discover all relevant pages.
*   **Robots.txt:** Configure `robots.txt` to guide search engine crawlers, preventing indexing of irrelevant or private pages (e.g., user profile).
*   **HTTPS:** All traffic will be served over HTTPS, a ranking factor and security standard.

## 5. Content Strategy for SEO

### 5.1. Blog & Tutorials
*   **Pillar Content:** Create comprehensive guides on fundamental knitting/crochet techniques, yarn types, or project planning.
*   **Regular Posts:** Publish consistent, high-quality articles and tutorials targeting specific long-tail keywords and answering common questions.
*   **"How-To" Guides:** Step-by-step instructions for various stitches, techniques, or small projects.
*   **Inspiration & Trends:** Articles on current craft trends, seasonal projects, or designer spotlights.
*   **Patterns:** Offer patterns as lead magnets, requiring registration, which also provides fresh, keyword-rich content.

### 5.2. Pattern Library
*   Ensure each pattern has a unique, SEO-friendly title and detailed description.
*   Categorize patterns effectively (e.g., by craft, difficulty, item type) to create clear navigation paths for search engines.

### 5.3. Portfolio Showcase
*   Each portfolio item should have a descriptive title and text explaining the project, techniques used, and inspiration.

## 6. Off-Page SEO

### 6.1. Backlink Building
*   **Guest Posting:** Contribute articles or patterns to other reputable craft blogs or websites, including a backlink to Nureaknite.
*   **Collaborations:** Partner with yarn brands, craft suppliers, or other designers for joint projects or content, leading to mutual backlinks.
*   **Resource Mentions:** Reach out to websites that compile craft resources or pattern libraries to get Nureaknite listed.
*   **Broken Link Building:** Identify broken links on relevant sites and suggest Nureaknite content as a replacement.

### 6.2. Social Media Engagement
*   Maintain active profiles on platforms like Instagram, Pinterest, and Facebook.
*   Share blog posts, new patterns, and product launches to drive traffic and generate social signals.
*   Engage with the community to build brand awareness and authority.

### 6.3. Brand Mentions
*   Monitor online mentions of "Nureaknite" and engage where appropriate.

## 7. Local SEO (for Offline Coaching)

*   **Google My Business (GMB):** Create and optimize a GMB profile for Nureaknite, including accurate business name, address, phone number, website, and business hours.
*   **Local Citations:** Ensure consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) information across online directories and listings.
*   **Location-Specific Keywords:** Integrate keywords like "knitting coach [city]," "crochet lessons [area]" into the offline coaching page and relevant blog content.
*   **Customer Reviews:** Encourage clients of offline coaching to leave reviews on Google and other platforms.

## 8. Measurement & Monitoring

*   **Google Analytics:** Track organic traffic, user behavior (bounce rate, time on page), conversion rates from organic channels, and popular content.
*   **Google Search Console:** Monitor keyword rankings, search impressions, click-through rates, indexing status, crawl errors, and Core Web Vitals.
*   **Keyword Rank Tracking Tools:** Regularly monitor the ranking performance of target keywords.
*   **KPIs:**
    *   Organic Search Traffic (growth month-over-month).
    *   Keyword Rankings (top 3, top 10 positions).
    *   Number of Indexed Pages.
    *   Backlink Profile Growth.
