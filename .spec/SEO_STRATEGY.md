# SEO_STRATEGY.md: Nurea Knit

## 1. SEO Goals

- Increase organic search visibility for relevant knitting, crochet, and craft-related keywords.
- Drive qualified organic traffic to the website, leading to increased engagement with content (blog, tutorials, patterns).
- Generate leads for coaching services.
- Establish Nurea Knit as an authoritative and trusted personal brand within the knitting and crochet community.

## 2. Keyword Strategy

### 2.1. Core Keyword Research

- **Informational:** "how to knit a scarf," "crochet stitches for beginners," "best yarn for blankets"
- **Navigational:** "Nurea Knit patterns," "Nurea Knit blog"
- **Commercial Investigation:** "easy knitting patterns free," "crochet kit for beginners"
- **Local:** "private knitting coach [location]"

### 2.2. Keyword Categories

- **Brand Keywords:** "Nurea Knit," "Nurea Knit patterns"
- **Craft-Specific Keywords:** "knitting patterns," "crochet tutorials," "yarn types"
- **Product/Service Keywords:** "crochet patterns free," "offline knitting coaching"
- **Long-Tail Keywords:** "how to fix dropped stitch knitting," "beginner friendly amigurumi pattern"

## 3. On-Page SEO

### 3.1. Content Optimization

- **Blog & Tutorials:** Each article targets specific keywords, structured with headings, bullet points, and internal links.
- **Pattern Library:** Each pattern page has unique title, meta description, and rich content including difficulty, craft type, materials.
- **Product Showcase:** Detailed descriptions using relevant keywords.
- **Portfolio Showcase:** Descriptions include technique, item type, design inspiration.
- **About Page:** Optimized with keywords related to "knitting designer," "crochet educator."

### 3.2. Technical On-Page Elements

- **Title Tags:** Unique, keyword-rich, max 60 characters.
- **Meta Descriptions:** Compelling summaries with target keywords, max 160 characters.
- **Header Tags:** Logical content hierarchy (H1, H2, H3).
- **Image Alt Text:** Descriptive alt text with relevant keywords.
- **URL Structure:** Clean, keyword-friendly (`/patterns/easy-crochet-scarf`).
- **Internal Linking:** 3+ links per post to related content.
- **Schema Markup:** Product (patterns), BlogPosting (blog), Organization (brand).

## 4. Technical SEO

- **Site Speed:** Next.js SSG/ISR, image optimization, Vercel CDN.
- **Mobile-Friendliness:** Fully responsive design.
- **Crawlability:** XML sitemaps, robots.txt, HTTPS enforced.
- **Core Web Vitals:** Monitor LCP, FID, CLS via Google Search Console.

## 5. Content Strategy for SEO

- **Pillar Content:** Comprehensive guides on fundamental techniques.
- **Regular Posts:** Consistent, high-quality articles targeting long-tail keywords.
- **Patterns as Lead Magnets:** Require registration, providing fresh keyword-rich content.

## 6. Off-Page SEO

- **Backlink Building:** Guest posting on craft blogs, collaborations, resource mentions.
- **Social Media:** Instagram, Pinterest, Facebook for traffic and social signals.
- **Brand Mentions:** Monitor and engage with online mentions.

## 7. Local SEO (Offline Coaching)

- Google My Business profile for offline coaching.
- Local citations with consistent NAP information.
- Location-specific keywords on coaching page.

## 8. Measurement & Monitoring

- **Google Analytics 4:** Track organic traffic, user behavior, conversions.
- **Google Search Console:** Monitor keyword rankings, impressions, CTR, Core Web Vitals.
- **KPIs:** Organic traffic growth, keyword rankings, indexed pages, backlink profile.
